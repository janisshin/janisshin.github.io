01 / ABOUT

At the intersection
of AI and biology.

I’m interested in turning complex scientific and healthcare data into tools people can use.

I earned my PhD in Molecular Engineering & Sciences at the University of Washington, where I studied metabolic network models, Bayesian inference, and automated biological annotation.

My work spans research and implementation: designing benchmarks for biological models, building LLM-powered annotation tools, and developing pipelines that turn lab reports into standardized biomarker data.
